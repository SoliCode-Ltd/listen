# Trying the iPhone app before it goes to Apple

For the person with the iPhone. It takes about 30 minutes. Tick each line, and for
anything that is not as described, take a screenshot (side button + volume up) and write
one line about what happened. Nothing here costs money: there is nothing to buy in the app.

These are the things that can only be seen on a real iPhone. Everything else was checked
on GitHub's Mac machines and on a local copy of the site.

**Before you start**: install **TestFlight** from the App Store, open the invitation
email on the iPhone, and install **Listen**. Use the test member you were given (not a
customer's account). Making a few short voices is expected and fine.

## 1. Opening

- [ ] The icon on the home screen is Listen's, with the name **Listen** (or **إسمع** when
      the phone is in Arabic).
- [ ] Opening the app shows the logo moving on white for a moment, then the sign-in page.
      No blank screen, no browser bar, no address bar.
- [ ] The sign-in page is in the phone's language the very first time the app is opened
      (English phone: English; Arabic phone: Arabic).
- [ ] Nothing is hidden under the notch or Dynamic Island at the top, or under the bar at
      the bottom of the screen.
- [ ] Turning the phone sideways does nothing (the app stays upright).

## 2. Signing in

- [ ] The sign-in page shows three ways: email and password, **Continue with Apple**,
      **Continue with Google**. The Apple button is not smaller or lower-contrast than the
      Google one.
- [ ] **Sign in with Apple** on a fresh account: Apple's own sheet comes up from the
      bottom; choose **Hide My Email**; you land in the app, signed in. No error.
- [ ] Sign out (Account tab), then **Continue with Google**: a sign-in sheet opens, and
      after choosing the account you are back in the app, signed in.
- [ ] Sign out, then sign in with the test member's email and password.

## 3. The question before a first voice

- [ ] The first time you open the **Studio** tab with a member who has not answered, a
      page asks before anything else: what is sent, who receives it, **Agree and
      continue** / **Not now**. Both buttons are visible without scrolling.
- [ ] **Not now** takes you to the Account page; the Studio stays closed and asks again.
- [ ] **Agree and continue** opens the Studio.
- [ ] Account tab → **How your text and voice are processed** shows the date you agreed
      and a way to withdraw.

## 4. Making and hearing a voice

- [ ] Studio: type one sentence, **Generate Voice Now**. The voice plays.
- [ ] While it plays, **lock the phone**. The sound continues. The lock screen shows
      Listen's player with the title and play/pause; pause and play work from there.
- [ ] Swipe down from the top-right corner (Control Centre): the same player is there.
- [ ] Leave the app (swipe up) while it plays: the sound continues.
- [ ] A phone call or another app's sound interrupts it, and nothing crashes.
- [ ] Flip the ring switch on the side to silent: a voice still plays.
- [ ] Delete a voice from History: the question that asks "are you sure" is in the page's
      language, with its two buttons in that language, and both buttons work.

## 5. Saving and sharing

- [ ] On a finished voice press **Save**: the phone's own "save to" sheet opens; save to
      "On My iPhone". Open the **Files** app: the audio file is there and plays.
- [ ] Press **Share**: the share sheet opens with the audio file; send it to yourself in
      any app; it arrives and plays.
- [ ] Cancelling either sheet shows no error message.

## 6. The microphone

- [ ] Studio → **Voice Cloning** → record: the phone asks for the microphone, with a
      sentence that explains why, **only now** (not when the app first opened).
- [ ] Allow, record ten seconds, stop: the recording can be played back.
- [ ] Agents tab → open the agent → **Talk to …**: you can speak with the agent and hear
      it answer. End the call.
      (Locking the phone during a call ends the listening: that is the phone's rule for
      the microphone, not a fault.)
- [ ] (Optional) Settings → Listen → turn the microphone off, try to record again: the app
      explains how to turn it back on instead of doing nothing.

## 7. Notifications

- [ ] When the app asks to send notifications, it explains why first; allow.
- [ ] Start a long voice (a long text), then leave the app. When the voice is ready, a
      notification arrives. Tapping it opens the app on that voice.
- [ ] No notification ever arrives that is an advertisement.

## 8. Links and shortcuts

- [ ] Send yourself `https://listenai.studio/en/dashboard` in Notes or Messages and tap
      it: it opens **in the Listen app**, not in Safari.
- [ ] A link to another site inside the app (for example in the privacy policy) opens in
      Safari, not inside the app.
- [ ] Press and hold the app's icon: **New voice-over**, **Voices**, **Voice agents**.
      Each opens the right page.

## 9. No money anywhere

Look at every tab and the Account page, signed out and signed in. Signed out, open the
menu at the top of the sign-in page and look at the voices and contact pages too.

- [ ] No price, no currency, no discount ("% off"), no plan to buy, no "Upgrade", no
      "Subscribe", no link that opens a payment page in Safari.
- [ ] No mention of Android or of another app store.

## 10. Deleting an account (use a throw-away account, not the test member)

- [ ] Create a new account with **Sign in with Apple**, then Account tab → **Delete my
      account** → confirm. It says the account is deleted and you are signed out.
- [ ] Signing in with Apple again creates a brand-new, empty account (the old one is gone).
- [ ] iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple: Listen is no longer in the list
      (or signing in asks again to share your name and email, which means the link was
      removed).

## 11. Odd moments

- [ ] Turn on Aeroplane Mode and open the app: its own "can't connect" screen shows, in
      the phone's language. Turn Aeroplane Mode off: the page loads by itself or with
      **Try again**.
- [ ] Pull down on a page: it refreshes.
- [ ] Swipe from the left edge: goes back a page.
- [ ] Typing in the text box: the keyboard does not cover what you are typing, and the bar
      of tabs is not floating above the keyboard.
- [ ] Switch the phone to **Arabic** (Settings → General → Language): the app opens in
      Arabic, right to left, and nothing is cut off.
- [ ] Press the sun/moon button at the top of a page: the page changes between light and
      dark, the colour behind the clock at the top follows it, and the choice is kept when
      the app is closed and opened again. (The app keeps Listen's own light or dark; it
      does not follow the phone's Dark appearance.)
- [ ] Largest text size (Settings → Display → Text Size): pages are still usable.
- [ ] Close the app completely and open it again: you are still signed in.

## 12. If there is an iPad at hand (optional)

- [ ] Install from TestFlight on the iPad: the app opens in iPhone size and the sign-in
      page works. (Apple's reviewers sometimes use an iPad.)
