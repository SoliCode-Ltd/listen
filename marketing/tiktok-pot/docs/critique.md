# «الحلة» TikTok ad: critique rounds

Scores 1–10: hook, readability at phone size, motion quality, variety, brand accuracy, sound sync.

## Round 1 (first build)
- **Scores:** hook 6, readability 6, motion 7, variety 8, brand 8, sync 7.
- **The three worst:**
  1. Frame 0 was empty: the hook must show something from the first frame.
  2. The type and chips were small on a phone.
  3. The studio moment was a phone screenshot in dark theme. The owner asked for the desktop studio in light theme, with real camera moves.
- **Fixed:**
  - Bigger avatar (r 235), chips (62 px) and lines (92 px).
  - The studio moment rebuilt from desktop captures.

## Round 2
- **Scores:** hook 7, readability 7, motion 8, variety 8, brand 8, sync 8.
- **The three worst:**
  1. Lines broke badly, leaving «ما» alone and the dialect lines on three rows.
  2. The conversation scene's faces were small, with empty space below.
  3. The owner asked for the studio how-to to show emotions and sound tags.
- **Fixed:**
  - Lines break where they read: before «قبل الغدا!».
  - Conversation faces at r 185.
  - The studio became a five-step how-to: dialect, typing, «[غاضب]», «<ضحكة>», generate, each with its caption.

## Round 3
- **Scores:** hook 8, readability 8, motion 8, variety 9, brand 9, sync 8.
- **The three worst:**
  1. The studio wasn't the page as users see it. The owner sent screenshots of live: header, side menu, voice library and the card.
  2. Two camera moves were too fast at high zoom and smeared.
  3. Frame 0 was still empty, because the first cue started at 0.16 s.
- **Fixed:**
  - Full-page captures at 1920 px, light theme, with the library filled («كل الأصوات 155», as on live), framed in a browser window.
  - Even zoom levels, and a pull-back in the middle of the long move to the button.
  - The first scene covers frame 0.

## Checks
- **The words:** every clip was transcribed against its script. One retake: the Egyptian mum said «وحدة» (Gulf), now «واحدة».
- **The final mix (main ad):** transcribed again, and every line is understood over the music.
- **Loudness:** the mix is at −14.1 LUFS, true peak −1.3 dBTP.
